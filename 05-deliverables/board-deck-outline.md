# Board Deck Outline

## Deliverable A
Board-level pack for a `Group Risk Committee` audience with limited technical depth but full fiduciary accountability.

## Required Shape
- `8–12` slides
- clarity and traceability over comprehensiveness
- precise risk statements

## Draft Slide Flow
1. Transaction context and why AI changes the risk profile
2. LumaPay at a glance and deal characteristics
3. AI systems in scope
4. Top `5–7` AI risks with lifecycle-stage and framework tags
5. Pre-close due-diligence view and evidence requests
6. Governance, operational, and cyber stress-test view
7. AI-specific cyber and monitoring enhancements
8. Risk-appetite and tolerance statements
9. `100-day` AI integration and governance plan
10. Standards traceability
11. Board decisions and escalation asks

## Slide 9 Guidance
- separate `day 0–30`, `31–60`, and `61–100`
- highlight lower-environment `PII` containment
- highlight `LumaCredit-EU` high-risk governance uplift
- highlight `MCP` permission reduction
- highlight vendor continuity and fallback planning
- highlight inherited-state versus target-state reporting
