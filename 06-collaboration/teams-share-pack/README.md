# Teams Or OneDrive Share Pack

Use this folder for share-friendly files once the drafts mature.

Recommended contents:
- PDF exports of the board deck and appendix
- Shareable `.docx` versions of narrative documents
- Final risk register in Excel or Markdown-to-Excel export
- A short `read-me-first` note for reviewers

Sharing guidance:
- Keep filenames stable and date-stamped only at major review points.
- Avoid duplicating working drafts here until they are ready for external group review.
