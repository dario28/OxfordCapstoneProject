# FraudShield

## Scenario Role
Third-party fraud-detection model illustrating `dependency` and `supply-chain` risk.

## Key Framework Anchors
- `ISO/IEC 27036`
- `DORA`
- `NIST CSF`

## Drafting Focus
- Third-party hosting and service boundaries
- Change-of-control and licence-assignment constraints
- Data-feed and model-output dependency risk
- Supplier assurance, incident rights, and audit rights
- Business continuity if the third-party relationship degrades
