# LumaCredit-EU

## Scenario Role
Credit-decision models whose `opacity`, `drift`, and `data-quality` characteristics require full lifecycle governance. Classified as `high-risk` under `EU AI Act Annex III`.

## Key Framework Anchors
- `EU AI Act`
- `ISO/IEC 42001`
- `ISO/IEC 5259`
- `NIST AI RMF`

## Drafting Focus
- Credit-decision flow and explainability needs
- Training and inference data quality controls
- Model monitoring and drift detection
- Cross-border data flow and privacy implications
- High-risk AI conformity and evidence expectations
