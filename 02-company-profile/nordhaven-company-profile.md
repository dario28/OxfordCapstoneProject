# Nordhaven Company Profile

## Company Overview
Nordhaven is the fictional acquiring firm. It is a larger, more mature financial-services organization using the acquisition to accelerate AI-enabled product growth and expand its digital credit capabilities.

## Corporate And Operating Profile
- Employee count: about `10,000`
- Enterprise maturity: established, control-oriented, and board-governed
- Operating model: multi-entity financial services organization with formal second-line and third-line functions
- Transaction posture: acquiring `80%` of LumaPay and inheriting its AI estate, vendors, and related liabilities

## Security And Risk Maturity
- Dedicated security team
- Dedicated `Governance, Risk, and Compliance` capability within security
- Established enterprise risk process
- More mature decision-rights and escalation model than LumaPay
- Better ability to impose common policy, control libraries, and reporting across the combined entity

## Tooling And Control Posture
- `CSPM` in place
- `DSPM` in place
- Modern AI security tools in place
- Likely stronger cloud governance, asset inventory, monitoring, and evidence retention than LumaPay

## Why Nordhaven Matters In The Scenario
Nordhaven creates the control benchmark in the case. The acquisition challenge is not whether mature governance exists somewhere in the combined estate, but whether Nordhaven can apply it fast enough to LumaPay’s AI-heavy environment without breaking operations or losing value.
