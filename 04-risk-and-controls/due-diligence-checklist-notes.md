# Due-Diligence Checklist Notes

## Structure
The checklist is organized across the five domains required by the capstone:
- data and IP
- model risk
- cyber
- regulation and ethics
- vendor and supply chain

## How To Use It
- Treat `Pre-close critical` items as diligence blockers or red flags to surface in Deliverable A.
- Treat `Post-close urgent` items as strong candidates for the `100-day` plan in Deliverable B.

## Best Pre-Close Items For The Board Pack
- live `PII` copied into `test`
- high-risk AI governance status for `LumaCredit-EU`
- `MCP` server inventory and permission scope
- change-of-control review for `FraudShield`
- cross-border trace and logging exposure

## Best Post-Close Items For The Roadmap
- AI-specific incident playbooks
- risk-appetite and tolerance statements
- FraudShield fallback testing
- board reporting improvements
