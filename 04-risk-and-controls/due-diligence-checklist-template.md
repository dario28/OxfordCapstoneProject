# AI Due-Diligence Checklist

| Domain | Question | Artefact To Request | Risk Addressed | Pre-Close Or Post-Close | Standard / Clause | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Data and IP | Has LumaPay copied live customer `PII` or financial data into `test` or other lower environments? | Environment inventory, sample dataset list, masking standard, exception log, retention settings | `R-01`, `R-06` | Pre-close critical | `GDPR` security of processing; `UK GDPR`; `ISO/IEC 27001` environment segregation | This should be treated as a day-1 red flag for Nordhaven. |
| Data and IP | What data lineage exists for `LumaCredit-EU` training, validation, and scoring datasets? | Data lineage diagrams, feature inventory, source register, label quality notes | `R-02`, `R-03` | Pre-close critical | `EU AI Act` data governance theme; `ISO/IEC 5259`; `NIST AI RMF` Map | Focus on decision data, bureau feeds, and open-banking inputs. |
| Data and IP | Do prompts, traces, or AI observability tools contain unminimized personal data? | LangSmith retention policy, trace samples, redaction settings, logging architecture | `R-06` | Pre-close critical | `GDPR`; `UK GDPR`; `NIST CSF` Detect | Relevant to both `LumaAssist Chat` and `AutoUnderwriter Agent`. |
| Data and IP | Are cross-border transfers into `US-hosted` cloud or SaaS services documented and justified? | Transfer map, subprocessors, transfer-impact assessment, DPA annexes | `R-06`, `GDPR-02`, `TPRM-03` | Pre-close critical | `GDPR` international transfers; `UK GDPR` | Include traces, support tools, vendors, and backups. |
| Data and IP | Is intellectual-property provenance clear for models, training data, prompts, and generated outputs? | Model/provider contracts, data-licence terms, policy notes on synthetic data | IP and provenance theme from scenario | Pre-close critical | `GDPR` accountability; company legal-compliance theme | Especially important for foundation models and generated content use. |
| Data and IP | Can LumaPay prove the licence and lineage basis for all training datasets, prompt templates, embeddings, synthetic data, and model outputs? | Data provenance register, licence register, prompt repository, model/provider terms, synthetic-data policy | `R-15` | Pre-close critical | `GDPR` accountability; `EU AI Act` GPAI and copyright transparency themes; `ISO/IEC 27036` | This strengthens the IP and provenance review beyond generic supplier contract checks. |
| Model risk | Is `LumaCredit-EU` formally classified as a high-risk credit-decision system and governed accordingly? | Model classification memo, risk assessment, approval record, control mapping | `R-02` | Pre-close critical | `EU AI Act Annex III`; `ISO/IEC 42001`; `NIST AI RMF` Govern | This is likely one of the most important diligence items in the pack. |
| Model risk | Are model validation, back-testing, and performance reviews complete for `LumaCredit-EU`? | Validation reports, performance dashboards, threshold settings, change approvals | `R-03` | Pre-close critical | `NIST AI RMF` Measure; `EU AI Act` robustness theme | Ask specifically for drift indicators and override trends. |
| Model risk | Does `AutoUnderwriter Agent` have documented boundaries for human oversight and no-auto-decision rules? | Agent design doc, manual-approval policy, override process, workflow screenshots | `R-07`, `R-09` | Pre-close critical | `EU AI Act` human oversight; `ISO/IEC 42001`; `NIST AI RMF` Govern | Needed to test whether the human-in-the-loop is real or nominal. |
| Model risk | Are prompt libraries, retrieval logic, and evaluation suites version-controlled and approved? | Prompt repository, evaluation results, release approvals, rollback procedure | `R-04`, `R-05`, `R-07` | Pre-close critical | `OWASP LLM Top 10`; `ISO/IEC 27001` secure development; `NIST AI RMF` Measure | Applies mainly to `LumaAssist Chat` and `AutoUnderwriter Agent`. |
| Model risk | Can the company explain how an underwriting or credit recommendation was produced? | Sample explanation records, trace bundles, reason-code documentation, case review pack | `R-02`, `R-09` | Pre-close critical | `ISO/IEC 42001`; `GDPR` accountability; `NIST AI RMF` Measure | Traceability will matter in both audit and board discussion. |
| Model risk | Do all four in-scope systems have current model cards or system cards? | Model cards or system cards for `LumaCredit-EU`, `LumaAssist Chat`, `AutoUnderwriter Agent`, and `FraudShield`; known-limitations statements; intended-use and out-of-scope-use notes | `R-02`, `R-09`, `R-14`, `R-16` | Pre-close critical | `EU AI Act` Article `11`; `ISO/IEC 42001` clause `8`; `NIST AI RMF` Map / Measure | Required to make the board pack defensible and to support the unified AI registry. |
| Model risk | Is there evidence that human review is meaningful rather than nominal for credit or underwriting decisions? | HITL procedure, reviewer training records, reviewer rationale samples, override-rate dashboard, escalation records | `R-07`, `R-17` | Pre-close critical | `EU AI Act` Article `14`; `GDPR` Article `22`; FCA Consumer Duty | Use override patterns and rationale quality to test for automation bias. |
| Model risk | Are fairness, discriminatory-outcome, and adverse-impact checks performed for `LumaCredit-EU`? | Fairness assessment, disparate-impact metrics, appeal and override trend analysis, protected-characteristic proxy review | `R-03`, `R-16` | Pre-close critical | `EU AI Act` Annex III 5(b), Articles `10`, `15`; `GDPR` Article `22`; `NIST AI RMF` Measure | This directly addresses unfair denial and biased credit outcome concerns. |
| Cyber | Have LLM workflows been tested for prompt injection, sensitive-data leakage, and insecure output handling? | Red-team report, penetration test scope, abuse-case test results, remediation log | `R-04`, `R-06`, `LLM-01`, `LLM-02` | Pre-close critical | `OWASP LLM Top 10`; `MITRE ATLAS`; `NIST CSF` Protect / Detect | Ask whether testing covered both production and test data paths. |
| Cyber | Are `MCP` servers inventoried and are tool permissions scoped by use case and environment? | MCP inventory, tool permission matrix, role mappings, environment separation notes | `R-05`, `R-08` | Pre-close critical | `ISO/IEC 27001` access control; `OWASP LLM Top 10`; `NIST CSF` Protect | One of the clearest ways to show agent risk in the appendix. |
| Cyber | Are credentials, API keys, and service roles for AI systems managed centrally and reviewed? | Secrets inventory, IAM role list, key-rotation standard, access review evidence | `R-05`, `R-08` | Pre-close critical | `ISO/IEC 27001`; `NIST CSF` Protect | Focus on Bedrock, MCP services, vendor APIs, and logging platforms. |
| Cyber | Are model, prompt, and tool invocation logs retained and reviewable for incident investigation? | Logging architecture, CloudTrail settings, Datadog dashboards, trace access logs | `R-06`, `R-09` | Pre-close critical | `ISO/IEC 27001` logging; `NIST CSF` Detect; `ISO/IEC 42001` | This is important for both operational and regulatory defensibility. |
| Cyber | Are decision, model, prompt, rule, and tool logs immutable or tamper-evident for high-impact workflows? | WORM or immutable logging evidence, model-version hash records, prompt-version history, change approvals, audit-integrity checks | `R-09`, `R-18` | Pre-close critical | `EU AI Act` Article `12`; `ISO/IEC 27001` Annex A `8.15`, `8.25`; `NIST CSF` Protect / Detect | Required to prove decisions are reconstructable and logs have not been altered. |
| Cyber | Are there documented incident-response playbooks for AI misuse, drift, vendor failure, and prompt abuse? | Incident playbooks, escalation matrix, tabletop results, contact tree | `R-10`, `R-12`, `NCSF-04` | Post-close urgent | `NIST CSF` Respond / Recover; `DORA` | Good candidate for day `0–30` in the roadmap if missing. |
| Regulation and ethics | Is there a unified AI governance structure or at least an interim operating model for the acquisition period? | Governance charter, RACI, board reporting template, risk forum terms of reference | `R-12`, `AIRMF-01`, `ISO42K-01` | Pre-close critical | `NIST AI RMF` Govern; `ISO/IEC 42001` | If not, Nordhaven inherits risk faster than it inherits control. |
| Regulation and ethics | Are risk appetite and tolerance statements defined for opacity, autonomy, dependency, drift, and scale asymmetry? | Risk appetite statement, board paper, KRIs, escalation thresholds | Risk-appetite requirement from assignment | Post-close urgent | `NIST AI RMF` Govern; `ISO/IEC 42001` | This is likely a board-pack deliverable rather than a pre-close artefact. |
| Regulation and ethics | Does LumaPay have a complete AI asset inventory, including shadow AI, informal tools, scripts, and departmental AI use? | AI asset inventory, shadow AI discovery evidence, department attestations, registry export, TSA assumptions | `R-12`, `R-13` | Pre-close critical | `NIST AI RMF` Map; `ISO/IEC 42001` clause `4`; `EU AI Act` Articles `6-7` | Absence means Nordhaven cannot size inherited regulatory exposure on day 1. |
| Regulation and ethics | Are board-level AI accountability, RACI, decision rights, and escalation paths documented? | Board AI policy, RACI, decision-rights matrix, escalation protocol, named accountability owner, statement of responsibilities if applicable | `R-02`, `R-07`, `R-12`, `R-17` | Pre-close critical | `NIST AI RMF` Govern; `ISO/IEC 42001` clauses `5.3`, `6.1`; UK corporate-governance accountability theme | This tests whether governance exists beyond named system owners. |
| Regulation and ethics | Are DPIAs, privacy assessments, or AI impact assessments completed for the high-risk or customer-facing systems? | DPIAs, AI impact assessments, legal review notes | `R-01`, `R-02`, `R-06` | Pre-close critical | `GDPR`; `EU AI Act` governance themes | Ask for both completed and draft assessments. |
| Regulation and ethics | Is responsibility for automated decision support documented across business, risk, legal, and engineering teams? | Decision-rights matrix, approval workflow, role descriptions | `R-02`, `R-07`, `R-12` | Pre-close critical | `ISO/IEC 42001`; `NIST AI RMF` Govern | Especially important for credit and underwriting systems. |
| Regulation and ethics | Does the board receive consistent, accurate reporting on inherited AI risks versus target-state plans? | Board packs, risk committee papers, integration risk reports | `R-12`, `AIRMF-05`, `INT-05` | Post-close urgent | `NIST AI RMF` Govern / Measure | We want inherited-state versus target-state reporting separated clearly. |
| Vendor and supply chain | Do vendor contracts include change-of-control terms, assignment rights, and termination triggers? | Vendor contracts, order forms, legal summary, assignment analysis | `R-10`, `TPRM-01` | Pre-close critical | `DORA`; `ISO/IEC 27036` | Highest priority for `FraudShield` and key data providers. |
| Vendor and supply chain | Do `FraudShield`, foundation-model, data-provider, observability, and MCP-adjacent vendor contracts include SLAs, audit rights, incident rights, fallback rights, and concentration-risk analysis? | Vendor contracts, SLAs, audit-rights schedules, assurance reports, incident-notification clauses, concentration-risk assessment, exit strategy | `R-10`, `R-11`, `R-15` | Pre-close critical | `DORA` Articles `28`, `30`; `ISO/IEC 27036`; `ISO/IEC 27001` supplier controls | This turns the change-of-control review into a broader operational-resilience review. |
| Vendor and supply chain | Are supplier security, resilience, and incident-rights expectations documented for critical AI and data vendors? | Security addenda, SLA, audit rights, assurance reports, incident history | `R-10`, `R-11`, `TPRM-02` | Pre-close critical | `ISO/IEC 27036`; `DORA`; `ISO/IEC 27001` supplier security | Applies to fraud vendor, bureau/data sources, and hosted observability tools. |
| Vendor and supply chain | Are subprocessor and hosting locations known for Bedrock-adjacent tools, traces, and observability services? | Vendor architecture summary, subprocessor list, DPA annexes, hosting statement | `R-06`, `TPRM-03` | Pre-close critical | `GDPR`; `UK GDPR`; `DORA` | Relevant to `LangSmith`, contact-center SaaS, and other support tooling. |
| Vendor and supply chain | Is there a tested fallback mode for `FraudShield` if service quality degrades or access is lost? | Fallback runbook, manual review plan, outage procedure, RTO / RPO notes | `R-10`, `R-11` | Post-close urgent | `NIST CSF` Recover; `DORA` | Useful for the roadmap and for demonstrating operational realism. |
| Vendor and supply chain | Are inherited MCP tools, AI plugins, or external APIs governed as formal third-party dependencies? | Third-party inventory, architectural dependency list, approval records | `AIRMF-02`, `INT-04`, `TPRM-05` | Pre-close critical | `NIST AI RMF` Map; `ISO/IEC 42001`; `ISO/IEC 27036` | This helps uncover shadow tools and hidden external reliance. |
